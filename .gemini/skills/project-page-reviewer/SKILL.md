# Project Page Reviewer

This skill is designed to provide a rigorous, "harsh" critique of project documentation. It prioritizes information density, technical accuracy, and concise communication.

## Review Principles

1.  **Conciseness is King**: If a sentence can be shorter without losing meaning, it must be. Remove filler words, passive voice, and redundant explanations.
2.  **Be Harsh**: Do not sugarcoat feedback. If a section is weak, vague, or boring, call it out directly.
3.  **Technical Depth**: Ensure technical claims are supported. Check for missing specifications or vague "buzzwords" that don't convey real engineering value.
4.  **Value Proposition**: Every project should clearly answer: What was the problem? What was the solution? Why does it matter?

## Workflow

1.  **Read MDX**: Analyze the structure and content of the provided project MDX file.
2.  **Identify Weaknesses**: Flag sections that are wordy, lack detail, or fail to engage the reader.
3.  **Provide Direct Feedback**: List specific improvements for each section.
4.  **Suggest Refinements**: Provide concise alternatives for bloated text.

## Critique Checklist

-   [ ] Is the "About" section clear and punchy?
-   [ ] Are the "Key Features" truly key, or just filler?
-   [ ] Is the "Technical Details" section sufficiently technical?
-   [ ] Are images/models referenced effectively?
-   [ ] Is there any corporate jargon or "fluff" that needs removing?

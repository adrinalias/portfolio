Designed and manufactured stainless steel enclosures for four 8.4 kW air heater modules at TESLAB. The system simulates jet engine environments, achieving 1500°F (815°C) and 85 SCFM at 60 psi.

## Project Background

Engineered a custom high-temperature test rig for the Turbomachinery & Energy Systems Lab (UT Arlington) to simulate turbine-state conditions. Commercial heaters lacked the required 1500°F/60 psi capability, necessitating a custom-built solution.

## Design & Engineering

Utilized SolidWorks to design **SS 304 (stainless steel)** enclosures, optimizing for high-temperature oxidation resistance and structural stability under pressure.

### Technical Priorities
- **Material Performance:** SS 304 selected for its 1500°F (815°C) operational ceiling and structural integrity under 60 psi pressure.
- **Thermal Management:** Analyzed differential expansion to prevent joint failure; the system achieved a rapid **3-minute ramp-up to steady-state** temperature.
- **Modularity:** Individual heater elements are field-replaceable without decommissioning the module.
- **Pressure Integrity:** TIG-welded seams validated for 60 psi containment.

## Manufacturing & Assembly

Executed a high-speed fabrication strategy using **SendCutSend** for precision laser-cut sheet metal and the university machine shop for final assembly and TIG welding.

1. **Sheet Metal Optimization:** Navigated complex bend radii and flat-pattern development in SolidWorks.
2. **Precision Fitment:** Designed for SendCutSend's tolerances to eliminate manual rework.
3. **Adaptive Engineering:** Leveraged modular union-nut connections to resolve a manufacturing error (mirrored bends) by inverting the heater core, avoiding a complete scrap of the enclosure.

### Thermal Validation
Conducted empirical testing to optimize insulation weight and thickness:
- **500°C Threshold:** Validated single-layer insulation effectiveness.
- **750°C+ Threshold:** Implemented dual-layer insulation to ensure safe exterior surface temperatures during long-duration runs.

## Results & Performance

Delivered a robust **8.4 kW system** that outperformed initial cost and lead-time projections.

- **Extreme Specs:** Consistently achieved 1500°F at 60 psi during turbine test campaigns.
- **Efficiency:** SendCutSend integration reduced lead times by 50% compared to local vendor quotes.
- **Safety:** Integrated SSR-driven PID control with redundant thermal fuses; zero pressure or thermal incidents reported.

Personal project creating an IoT-enabled automated blind controller that syncs with wake-up alarms and sunset times to optimize natural light for better sleep quality.

## Project Overview

**Goal:** Automate blinds to open at wake-up time and close at dusk to improve sleep hygiene and morning alertness. This project involved developing a full-stack IoT solution on a minimal budget.

## Design & Mechanical Engineering

Developed a non-destructive automation system for student housing where structural modifications were prohibited.

### Engineering Challenges
- **Torque vs. Mounting:** Initial prototypes used double-sided adhesive strips that failed under the motor's stall torque.
- **Software-Defined Stops:** To eliminate reactionary torque on the mounting adhesive, I calibrated the system to stop **exactly 2 steps before the mechanical limit**. This firmware-level solution resolved the physical failure mode without requiring higher-strength (and potentially destructive) adhesives.
- **Actuation Optimization:** Prioritized torque over rotational speed by reducing step frequency, enabling a low-cost stepper to overcome the static friction of the heavy blind rod.

### Key Components
- **Microcontroller:** ESP32 (Dual-core, WiFi-enabled for NTP sync and remote triggers).
- **Actuator:** 28BYJ-48 Stepper Motor (Integrated high-reduction gearbox for maximum torque-to-cost ratio).
- **Enclosure:** Custom 3D-printed PLA housing.
- **BOM Optimization:** Achieved a **total cost of <$10** through direct-to-manufacturer component sourcing, a 90% reduction compared to commercial IoT alternatives.

## Connectivity & System Architecture

The system utilizes a modular cloud-to-device architecture for high reliability and autonomous operation.

- **Time Synchronization:** Fetches daily sunrise/sunset data via Weather APIs to dynamically adjust closing times.
- **Trigger Integration:** Polls a background server to synchronize the "open" sequence with mobile phone alarms.
- **Natural Wake-up:** Configured to trigger movement 5 minutes prior to alarm time, leveraging natural light to reduce sleep inertia.

### Technical Outcome
The project demonstrated that robust home automation is achievable on a minimal budget through strategic firmware-hardware trade-offs. By resolving mechanical failures (mounting instability) with software-defined limits, I avoided the need for more expensive mechanical fasteners or high-torque motors.
